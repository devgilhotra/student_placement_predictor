Q1) What did I find during EDA?

--> I found that internship experience was positively associated with placement. The observed placement rate increased from about 62.8% for students with no internships to 77.2% for students with three internships.



CGPA ↑ → Placement rate ↑
Internships ↑ → Placement rate ↑
Coding skills ↑ → likely placement ↑


Placed students have slightly higher average DSA, aptitude, and communication scores than non-placed students, suggesting these variables may provide useful predictive information.


Correlation does not mean causation, and it doesn't tell us that one feature is automatically more important to the final Logistic Regression model. It's an exploratory measure.


CGPA has a positive linear association with placement, but CGPA alone does not explain placement very well.
